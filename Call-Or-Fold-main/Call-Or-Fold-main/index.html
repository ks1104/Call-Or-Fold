The four betting rounds

The deal

Everyone gets two private cards. The dealer button moves one seat left each hand. The player left of the button posts the small blind, the next posts the big blind. Heads-up, the button posts the small blind and acts first before the flop.

Who is who

Each seat is badged with its position. BTN is the dealer button, the best seat — it acts last on every street after the flop. SB and BB are the small and big blind, badged in red because they have already put money in before seeing their cards. UTG is under the gun, first to act and the worst seat. HJ and CO (hijack and cutoff) sit between.

The button moves one seat left after every hand, so everyone pays the blinds equally often. Heads-up the button posts the small blind and its badge reads SB · BTN.

The four streets

Preflop — bet on your two cards. Flop — three shared cards. Turn — a fourth. River — a fifth. A betting round follows each one.

Your options

Check — stay in for free when no one has bet. Bet — put chips in first. Call — match the current bet. Raise — increase it. Fold — give up the hand.

No limit

You can bet anything from the minimum up to your whole stack. A raise must be at least as big as the last bet or raise. If you run out of chips you are all in — you can still win the part of the pot you paid for, and the rest becomes a side pot.

Showdown

Best five cards win, using any mix of your two and the five on the table. Ties split the pot.
